HotspotPro

See how much data your Personal Hotspot has shared, who is using it, and cap them.

Add this repo in Sileo, Zebra or Cydia:

@BASE_URL@/

Add to Sileo

What it adds

  • Data used this period, with a monthly reset day you choose
  • Who is connected — name, address, and how much each device used
  • A limit that warns you, and per-device limits that cut a device off

Everything appears inside the stock Settings → Personal Hotspot pane. Free, open source, MIT licensed.

Compatibility

iOS 14+, rootless and rootful, arm64 and arm64e. Tested on iOS 16.7.15 (iPhone 8 Plus, Dopamine).
