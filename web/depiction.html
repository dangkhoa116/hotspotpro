What it adds

Personal Hotspot tells you almost nothing: no data total, no list of who is connected, no cap. HotspotPro adds all three, inside the stock Settings → Personal Hotspot pane.

  • Data used this period, with a monthly reset day you choose
  • Who is connected — name, address, and how much each device used
  • A limit that warns you, and per-device limits that cut a device off

Privacy

Everything stays on your device; nothing is uploaded. To attribute bytes to devices, a background helper reads packet headers on the tethering bridge — 14 bytes per frame, the Ethernet header, never packet contents. It stores client MAC addresses, the names devices announce over DHCP, and byte counts. Records are forgotten after 45 days, and switching Track Hotspot Usage off closes the tap entirely.

Compatibility

iOS 14+, rootless and rootful, arm64 and arm64e.
Tested on iOS 16.7.15 (iPhone 8 Plus, Dopamine).
